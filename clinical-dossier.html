Section 2.7.3: Summary of Clinical Efficacy

Investigational Product: Lerocitinib Mesylate (INF-702) · Study INF-702-03

Executive Efficacy Overview: Clinical Study INF-702-03 is a multinational, randomized (1:1), double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III trial evaluating Lerocitinib Mesylate (400 mg daily) in combination with standard chemotherapy in 1,420 adult patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer.
